# Thesis

The deepest thesis is becoming:

Intelligence should move through relationships without acquiring sovereignty over them.

Everything else can be derived from that.

Derived here:

1. A model can be powerful without owning the place.
2. A node can execute without becoming the authority.
3. A ledger can prove an event without becoming the entire application.
4. Providers supply capability, never sovereignty.
5. Something else can work on a Thing without owning the Thing, the Place, or the Human. (HANDOFF 003)
