15-second continuation of the previous cinematic technology sequence, 16:9.

FIRST FRAME: an abstract glowing geometric diamond completely fills the frame. Camera continues moving forward through its center.

The diamond opens into an obsidian 3D design studio. One elegant abstract 3D object floats in the center while translucent technical interfaces orbit around it.

The same object is shown simultaneously through four different interface representations: desktop workstation, tablet drafting surface, mobile control surface and terminal engineering view.

The interfaces separate into geometric pattern pieces connected by fine golden threads. Technical annotations, node graphs, equations, bounding boxes and lineage diagrams animate around them like a living CAD blueprint.

The diagrams begin growing organically. Directory-tree lines transform into roots. Circuit traces become branches. Golden data threads become veins.

Camera pulls dramatically backward to reveal that the entire software architecture forms an enormous abstract golden world tree suspended in black space.

The tree remains partly technological: coordinate markers, node points, hashes, wireframes and tiny terminal windows remain visible among its branches.

End on the golden tree, then rapidly collapse everything to black with one small blinking terminal cursor.

Visual style: 1990s punk software culture fused with precision CAD, old internet graphics, CRT phosphor, photocopied engineering diagrams, black glass, restrained cyan and metallic gold. Sophisticated, strange, tactile, engineered.

Sound: keyboard percussion becomes rhythmic mechanical clicks, floppy-drive seek, sampler kick, electrical pulse, then sudden silence.
