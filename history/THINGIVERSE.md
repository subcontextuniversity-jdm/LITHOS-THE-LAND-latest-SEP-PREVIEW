# Thingiverse

People made things.

Somebody had an idea, modelled it, uploaded files. Somebody else printed them. Maybe it failed. They changed it. Remixed it. Uploaded again. Ancestry. Branches. Community.

Not yet "a distributed object-lineage operating system." Probably just trying to make cool shit. That matters. The grammar came later so the commons would not have to become a corporation to stay a commons.

A Thing needs to survive the application that created it.

Gator is allowed to work on such a Thing.
Gator is not allowed to become Thingiverse.
