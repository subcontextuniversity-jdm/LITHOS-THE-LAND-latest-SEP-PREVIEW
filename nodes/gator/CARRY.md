# Carry

Gator may carry a Thing **within the place**.

`carryWithinPlace(session, thing)` 

- requires grant `carry_within_place`
- refuses a different `thingId`
- does not take the Thing home as property
- does not take the human's past as cargo

Carry is TALON. Sovereignty is not.

The Golden Tree said agents can carry. This file is where that sentence is made small enough to test.
