# Pressure

Airlocks exist because two atmospheres should meet without dumping one into the other.

Here the atmospheres are:

| Side | Pressure |
| --- | --- |
| Human | Authority, memory that is theirs, the right to knot |
| Node | Capability, scope, the duty to leave a receipt |

Equalize means:

- both may inhabit the **same place**
- both may touch the **same Thing** under grants
- neither becomes the other

If the node inherits the human's knot, the human-side atmosphere has dumped into the machine.
If the human must speak only through the node, the machine-side atmosphere has dumped into the person.

VG06 applies to pressure as well: the system can map the past (Sentinel), but it cannot carry it for you (no node inherits your memory as if it were a soul).
