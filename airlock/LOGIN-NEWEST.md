# LOGIN-NEWEST

SEP-LATEST login. Not OAuth theatre. Not fourteen screens.

## Walk

1. **Arrive.** A name. CURSOR = HUMAN.
2. **Present node.** `GATOR` or `SENTINEL`. Capability credential, not a person.
3. **Split.** Two doors. See `SPLIT-GATE.md`.
4. **Equalize.** Place + Thing + grants, minus forbidden glyphs, plus weight from scars already on the Thing.
5. **Work.** Gator may execute. Sentinel may map and attest.
6. **Knot.** Human only.
7. **Receipt.** Hash. Home.

`loginNewest({ name, nodeKind, thing, grants })` in `src/airlock.mjs` is this walk compressed for tests and the browser proof.

## What is new

Older software treated login as "prove you are the account that owns the app."

LOGIN-NEWEST treats login as "prove which kind of subject you are, then borrow a place."

The human does not become ten disconnected accounts because ten providers drew fences.
The node does not become the human because it was convenient for the protocol.
